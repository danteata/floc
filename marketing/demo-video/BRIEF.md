## Intent

A demo church leaders can watch muted, in under a minute and a half, and come
away knowing what Floc does for their church: members check themselves in on
Sunday, leaders see the week at a glance, Floc points out who has stopped
coming, the absent list goes to the people who will phone them, and giving is
recorded alongside. Warm and pastoral, never salesy. Plain words a church
secretary would use.
